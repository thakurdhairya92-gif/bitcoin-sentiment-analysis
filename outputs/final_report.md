# Bitcoin Market Sentiment Analysis

## Objective

To analyze the relationship between trader performance and Bitcoin market sentiment using Hyperliquid trading data and the Fear & Greed Index.

---

## Datasets Used

1. Historical Trader Data
2. Fear & Greed Index

---

## Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- VS Code
- Jupyter Notebook

---

## Analysis Performed

- Data cleaning
- Date conversion
- Dataset merging
- Sentiment-based analysis
- Profit analysis
- Trade frequency analysis
- Visualization creation

---

## Key Findings

- Trader behavior changes significantly during fear and greed periods.
- Market greed is associated with higher trading activity.
- Closed PnL shows volatility during extreme sentiment conditions.
- Market psychology impacts decision-making patterns.

---

## Conclusion

Market sentiment plays a critical role in trader behavior and profitability. Understanding emotional cycles can help improve trading strategies and risk management.
